# Junyi (Stanley) Ye

Data analyst working on predictive modeling, risk and product analytics, experimentation, and analytics engineering, and building production LLM systems with automated evaluation.

- **Now:** regional sales analytics at a consumer products distributor: a reporting standard used by three regions, churn early warning, account-level forecasting, and a dbt model layer.
- **Building:** [Piaopiao Travel](https://jyye.org/work/piaopiao), an AI travel-planning product with tool-calling agents, LLM-as-judge evaluation, and a fine-tuned extraction model.
- **Background:** B.A. in Applied Mathematics and Data Science, UC Berkeley · M.A. in Public Policy Studies, University of Chicago.
